The caller supplies an authorized DHS IR file. Preparation validates the input and

## Definitions and assumptions

Modern-method discontinuation excludes EC. EC remains in all-women mCPR and
counts as modern use in endpoint sensitivities. Potentially actionable causes are
method failure, wanting a more effective method, bleeding changes, other side
effects/health, and access/cost/convenience. Partner/spousal disapproval is separate
and excluded. Other endings compete rather than being deleted from risk sets.

Main planning treats every actionable original-method ending as a lost modern
user. It contains first-year, historical duration-aware and AFT approaches.
First-year planning uses all 8,682 eligible non-EC modern episodes; the national
planning sample is not restricted to regression complete cases. Historical and AFT
approaches retain their documented duration-specific samples.

Three switching sensitivities are separate: historical landmarks, AFT endings with
observed pathways, and AFT endings with modeled subsequent transitions. The new
model estimates immediate post-ending status categorically and uses AFT-based
competing transition hazards thereafter. It predicts modern use at the common
next-12-month endpoint, not everyone who ever restarted after an ending. Destination
modern methods are pooled. Clock-reset transition families are selected at the
point fit and held fixed for uncertainty calculations.

## Validation and limitations

The point fit and all 500 joint positive-multiplier PSU replicates passed count/risk
identities and sensitivity bounds. The full IR frame includes 797 PSUs in 27 strata;
all linked episodes and months from a woman share her PSU multiplier. Current-use
composition, discontinuation estimates and pathways are re-estimated jointly.
Displayed planning cells, method/cause totals, scenario additions and pp conversions
were checked against the same numerical vectors. The incremental R rebuild matches
all saved HTML tables and retains its figures. AFT fitted-output inconsistencies
previously identified in the saved report have been reconciled.

mCPR pp contributions equal retained users divided by 35,016,171 women aged 15–49
in 2026, multiplied by 100. The baseline stock uses 34,057,556 women in 2025 and
survey-weighted all-women mCPR. The same reference cohort is retained across the
6-year DHS trend, 9-year DHS trend and FPET-informed growth scenarios. The last
extends a published increment from a DHS anchor; it is not an official FPET forecast.

Intervals condition on fixed population, growth rates and selected model families.
They do not fully capture recall error, model selection, future transportability,
sparse-cell instability or the effect of a specific intervention. CI endpoints do
not add across cells. Earlier exploratory HTML blocks use different reason scopes
or duration groupings and are labeled accordingly. Unchanged earlier analytical
chapters are retained, not freshly re-estimated by the incremental report build.
