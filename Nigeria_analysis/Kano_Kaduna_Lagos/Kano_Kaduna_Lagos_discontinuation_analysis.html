METHODS

Definitions and precision

The unit for discontinuation is an uninterrupted episode of a modern method. A woman can contribute more than one episode. Non-use, pregnancies and births are not method-use episodes. The DHS observation window is 3–62 months before interview, with right censoring and duration-specific delayed entry retained. Each monthly rate uses episodes still at risk at that month.

All-cause risk is one minus survival. Actionable risk is the sum of five competing-risk cumulative incidences. Every other method ending—including wanted pregnancy and partner/spousal disapproval—remains a competing event. These are original-method endings; switching to another modern method can still count as ending the original method.

The five-year all-reason distributions use recorded modern-method endings of any duration in the 60-month calendar window. The actionable composition uses first-year cause-specific cumulative incidence. These answer different questions and use different denominators.

mCPR is current modern use (v313=3) among all women ages 15–49. It includes EC, consistent with the modern-use definition. EC is excluded from continuing-use life tables and recorded modern-method ending distributions. Initiation mix includes all observed starts, including EC and non-modern methods. Other modern combines condoms, sterilization, LAM, SDM and other recorded modern methods; it excludes the separately displayed pill, IUD, injectable and implant categories.

Point estimates use v005 weights. The 95% intervals use 999 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates of PSUs within v022 strata, drawn from the full survey frame, including PSUs without eligible episodes. All episodes within a woman retain the same PSU multiplier. No state pairwise differences are tested here.

The main discontinuation analysis pools modern methods, excluding EC. The appendix contains exploratory method-specific results. DHS normally suppresses estimates below 125 month-1 exposures and flags 125–249. We additionally omit extremely sparse estimates with fewer than 25 month-1 exposures or five first-year endings. Pooled estimates still require confidence intervals, and formal state contrasts have not been tested.
